Subject: candid people in real advisory moments — accountants with clients, teams at a table, over-the-shoulder at a laptop. Warm natural daylight, business-casual to formal, approachable. Avoid: cold blue cast, heavy filters, dark low-key light, empty stock cliché.

The rule: transparent only when the image is anchored to a section seam.
• Anchored (a figure that stands on the boundary between two sections) — cut out on a transparent background, no frame. Top starts at the section's content margin (level with the eyebrow); bottom sits flush with the edge of the next section. Keep a real gutter (~64px) to the text. Deliver on a plain white background, composed bottom-anchored.
• Not anchored, light section — a normal photo in a 16px rounded frame with a 1px hairline and a soft shadow, contained in its column with normal margins. Fills the frame; no transparency.
• Dark / forest section — contained and centred on an opaque tile: 1px hairline, the top-right corner cut on a short diagonal, art inside ~16px padding.
