# Sokndall — copy request: two FAQ headlines

From: design · To: copywriting
Two items: **F-1** (`/landing`) and **F-2** (`/pricing`)
Same response format as before: APPROVE with final text, or REJECT with the reason.

## Why

Both FAQ sections were an eyebrow pill and nothing else — no headline. The founder's
note: *"pasa muy desapercibido por su titular o su ausencia de él, porque el titular
está en formato badge lo cual por sí solo no causa peso visual."* He is right, and it
is the same finding that produced the six eyebrows in the last round, inverted: a pill
labels a section, it cannot *be* the section's headline.

The two sections were also labelled inconsistently — `/landing` said **FAQ** and
`/pricing` said **Questions**. Both now say **FAQ**; that part is settled and needs no
copy from you.

**Both pages are currently running a provisional headline I wrote so the layout is not
broken while this is open.** They are marked `HEADLINE IS PROVISIONAL` in the source and
should be replaced by whatever you approve — do not treat them as a starting point you
have to stay near.

## F-1 · `/landing` — FAQ section headline

**Running provisionally:** "Questions we get"

**What the section actually holds** — six items, and they are not price questions, they
are *permission-to-proceed* questions: do I have to talk to anyone, what happens on day
15, is my patient data safe, we already have a spreadsheet why change, does this work
for behavioral health, what if I go over the limit.

**Constraints:** four to eight words. Sits under the eyebrow **FAQ**, above the
accordion, left-aligned at the page margin. Same register as the other section headlines
on the page (*The renewals are not the hard part. The waiting is.* / *Three things,
tracked in one place* / *What Sokndall does not do*). A two-tone split is available —
the second half sets in forest green — but is not required.

## F-2 · `/pricing` — FAQ section headline

**Running provisionally:** "Questions about the price"

**What the section actually holds** — the brief calls this section *FAQ (objections)*
and that is exactly what it is: is there a contract, is there an annual discount, what
if I go over my limit, what counts as a provider, can I export everything, do you offer
setup help. Every one is a reason someone might not buy, answered flatly.

**Constraints:** same as F-1. It closes the page whose whole argument is that nothing is
hidden and there is no negotiation — the headline can carry that, and it should not
duplicate the H1 (*Credentialing software pricing, published*).

**One note worth your judgement:** the two headlines do not have to be parallel. These
are different pages doing different jobs, and matching them for the sake of matching
would be the weaker choice if each reads better on its own.
