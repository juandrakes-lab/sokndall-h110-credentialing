# Imagery

The reference comp is a screenshot, so no usable photo crops could be lifted from
it. These two files are **placeholders** at the right aspect ratios:

| File | Used in | Aspect |
|---|---|---|
| `hero-team.svg` | hero, right ~62% | 720 × 560 |
| `about-desk.svg` | "Simplifying Accounting" block | 620 × 460 |

## Replacing them

Drop real photos in this folder. Keep the same base names (any extension) and
update the references in `ui_kits/marketing-site/*.jsx` and
`templates/marketing-homepage/`.

## Direction

See `guidelines/imagery.html`. In short: candid people in real advisory moments,
warm natural daylight, business-casual to formal, 14px corners, one corner may be
clipped square. Avoid cold colour casts, heavy filters, and empty stock cliché.
