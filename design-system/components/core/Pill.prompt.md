One-line: the label that sits above almost every heading in the system.

```jsx
<Pill icon="🏆">Top-Rated Accounting &amp; Advisory Firm in USA</Pill>
<Pill icon="💛" tone="dark">We're Here To Support Your Financial Success</Pill>
```

Exactly one leading glyph. Keep the text to one short phrase. Not uppercase, not tracked-out — that is the difference from a conventional eyebrow.
