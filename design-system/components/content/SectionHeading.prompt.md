One-line: the eyebrow + headline that opens each section.

```jsx
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="We're Here To Support Your Financial Success" eyebrowIcon="💛" tone="dark" align="center"
  title="Comprehensive Accounting Solutions for Your Business." />
```

Headlines are Title Case, 4–7 words, one colour (no accent split). On forest sections pass `tone="dark"`.
