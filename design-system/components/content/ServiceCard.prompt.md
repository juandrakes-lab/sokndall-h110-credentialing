One-line: one service in the 2×2 (or 4-up) services grid.

```jsx
<ServiceCard icon="receipt" title="Tax Planning &amp; Preparation"
  body="Strategic tax planning and accurate filing to minimize your liability and keep you compliant year-round." />
```

Bodies are one to two sentences. On the forest section keep the default `tone="dark"`; use rows of two on desktop with `--card-gap`.
